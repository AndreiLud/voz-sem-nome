Portfolio project

Climate survey and eNPS analysis with local AI

Voz sem nome removes names, documents and contact details from survey comments before any model reads the text. Then a language model running on the server itself groups the comments by theme, measures sentiment and flags cases that need HR attention, such as harassment and burnout.

Try the demo See the dashboard

The problem

In a climate survey, the score says little. The reasons are in the open comments, and someone has to read them one by one.

  1. Manual reading

    Reading, sorting by theme and counting comments takes HR days in every cycle. With hundreds of answers, the analysis arrives late.

  2. Privacy risk

    Comments mention coworkers, managers, branches and sometimes a phone number or email. Whoever reads them learns who is being discussed, and a small slice of data can reveal who answered.

  3. Serious signals get lost

    A report of harassment or exhaustion can sit among the other answers until the final report.

Context. Since 26 May 2026, Brazil's NR-1 regulation includes psychosocial risk factors in the occupational risk management that companies must run. A climate survey does not meet that requirement on its own, but open comments often show these risks early. This is context, not legal advice.

The solution

A flow that treats every comment as sensitive data, from submission to dashboard.

  • Anonymization first

    Rules and a small name recognition model replace personal data with placeholders such as PESSOA and CPF. The original text is never stored.

  • AI on the server itself

    Classification runs on an open model with Ollama, on the server's CPU. No comment goes to a third party API.

  • Dashboard with a minimum group

    No group with fewer than 5 answers is shown. Small areas are merged so nobody can be identified by subtraction.

  • Critical case alerts

    When the model or the risk lexicon points to harassment, discrimination, burnout or threats, n8n records the alert and can notify the person in charge on Telegram, with the anonymized text only.

How it works

Every answer goes through four kinds of parts. The legend shows what is a fixed rule, what is a small name recognition model, what is a language model and what is automation.

Legend

  • RULE Fixed rule: patterns, word lists and check digits. Always gives the same result.
  • NER Small model: recognizes names of people and places with spaCy.
  • LLM Language model: reads the anonymized text and returns labels in a fixed format.
  • N8N Automation: n8n workflows that notify, summarize and clean up.

The diagram as text

  1. Answer The answer arrives with a score from 0 to 10, area, tenure and an optional comment.
  2. Personal data rules RULE Patterns and check digits find Brazilian documents (CPF, CNPJ, RG, CEP), email addresses, phone numbers, plates and employee IDs. A list of names from IBGE, the Brazilian statistics office, helps find personal names.
  3. Name recognition NER A small spaCy model finds names of people and places the rules miss. Each finding becomes a placeholder such as PESSOA. The original text is dropped here.
  4. Risk lexicon RULE Explicit phrases about harassment, violence or self-harm flag the case as critical, even if the model fails.
  5. Local language model LLM The gemma4:e2b-it-qat model, running on Ollama, returns themes, sentiment and a first critical flag.
  6. Second reading LLM With any sign of risk, the model reads the comment again, focused on critical cases. When in doubt, the case stays flagged.
  7. Dashboard The dashboard shows only groups with 5 or more answers.
  8. n8n N8N n8n deletes public submissions after 30 days and monitors the queue. With Telegram turned on, it also sends the alerts and a daily summary.

How the calculation works
  • Comments per year = answers × share with a comment × cycles.
  • Manual work = comments × (reading minutes + anonymization minutes) ÷ 60.
  • Review with the system = comments × reviewed share × minutes per review ÷ 60.
  • Hours saved = manual work minus review. Machine time uses the measured mean per comment and does not count as people's work.

Privacy

The demo was designed to keep as little as possible.

  • The original comment text is never stored. Only the anonymized version is.
  • IP addresses are never stored. They are used only in memory, to limit excessive submissions.
  • Public submissions are deleted after 30 days.
  • Groups with fewer than 5 answers are not shown on the dashboard.
