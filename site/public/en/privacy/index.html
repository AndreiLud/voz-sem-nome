Privacy

This page explains what the public demo collects and how each item is handled. It is a portfolio project and uses a fictitious company.

What the demo collects

  • The score from 0 to 10, the area and the tenure you pick.
  • The comment, if you write one. It is anonymized before it is stored.
  • The page language and the date and time of the submission.
  • The result of the bot check (Cloudflare Turnstile). The token is verified and dropped.

What is anonymized and how

Before storing anything, the system looks for personal data in the comment and replaces each item with a placeholder. Fixed rules find documents and contact details by their patterns and check digits. A list of names from IBGE, the Brazilian statistics office, and a small name recognition model find people and places. Only the text with placeholders goes on to the language model.

Placeholders used instead of personal data
PlaceholderWhat it replaces
[PESSOA]person's name
[LOCAL]place
[EMAIL]email address
[TELEFONE]phone number
[CPF]CPF, Brazilian taxpayer number
[CNPJ]CNPJ, Brazilian company number
[RG]RG, Brazilian ID card
[CEP]CEP, Brazilian postal code
[MATRICULA]employee ID
[PLACA]license plate
[DOCUMENTO]other document
[ofensivo]swear word, in public excerpts

No method is always right. That is why we ask you not to write real data. In public excerpts on the dashboard, swear words also become a placeholder.

What is never stored

  • The original comment text.
  • Your IP address. It stays in memory only, to limit excessive submissions and to count distinct people per day with a code that changes every day.
  • Tracking cookies or audience analytics. The site uses none.

How long data is kept

  • Public submissions: deleted after 30 days.
  • Alert log: only an identifier, the case types and timestamps, deleted after 90 days.
  • Daily counters without any text, such as number of submissions and analysis time: kept for the production statistics.

Minimum group

No group, cross tab or slice with fewer than 5 answers is shown on the dashboard. Small areas are merged into one unnamed group. When that group is small too, the smallest visible area joins it, so nobody can find a hidden group by subtracting the others from the total.

Suggested legal basis for a real company

In this demo, the basis is your consent, given with the confirmation box on the form. In a real company in Brazil, the most common basis for a climate survey is legitimate interest (LGPD, art. 7, IX), with a documented balancing test and the right not to answer. Comments may contain sensitive data, such as health or sexual orientation, which needs a basis from art. 11. When the survey supports the psychosocial risk management required by NR-1, compliance with a legal obligation may be considered.

This is a portfolio project and not legal advice. A real company should confirm the legal basis with its data protection officer and legal team.
